Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Effective immediately

Margin Tarot (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a tarot companion for iPhone. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling any data with care. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Margin Tarot, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Summary

Margin Tarot is designed to keep your personal reflections private. All journal entries, custom cards, and reading history are stored locally on your device. We do not require an account and we do not collect personal information directly. The only data that leaves your device is the limited, anonymized usage data described below — used solely to measure advertising performance — and only if you grant tracking permission.

2. Data We Store Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your device and never transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Your journal entries and saved readings
  • Custom cards you create (including any photos you import)
  • Your app preferences (e.g., reversed cards, haptic feedback, daily card state)

You can clear all of this data at any time from Settings → Data → Reset Everything.

3. Tracking & Advertising Disclosure

Tracking Declaration

Margin Tarot uses tracking. The app integrates the Adjust SDK, which may collect device identifiers and usage information to measure advertising performance and attribution. Tracking is gated by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework: the first time the app is launched on iOS 14.5 or later, you will see Apple's standard permission prompt. You may choose “Allow Tracking” or “Ask App Not to Track.” You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3.1 What tracking means in this app

When you grant tracking permission, the Adjust SDK collects a limited set of identifiers and event data from your device so we (and our advertising partners) can attribute installs and measure campaign performance. This data is associated with your device and may be considered “tracking” under Apple's rules and other privacy regulations.

3.2 Data collected for tracking purposes

3.3 Tracking domains

Tracking activity originates from the following domains, as declared in our app's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest:

  • adjust.com
  • attribution.Adjust.io

3.4 If you decline tracking

If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the Adjust SDK will still run in a non-tracking mode. It may receive anonymized, aggregated signals for basic install measurement, but it will not access the IDFA or other tracking identifiers, and it will not link activity to a device profile for advertising purposes. The app remains fully functional.

3.5 How to change your tracking choice

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Margin Tarot, then toggle Allow Tracking on or off.
  • Reset all apps: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Ask Apps to Stop Tracking.

4. Permissions We Request

Margin Tarot requests only the permissions it needs to work. Each is gated by Apple's system prompt and is optional:

  • Camera — to let you capture your own physical tarot cards and add them to your personal deck. (You can decline and still use every other feature.)
  • Photo Library (read) — so you can import card artwork or reference photos into your custom deck.
  • Photo Library (add) — to save reading snapshots back to your photo library when you choose to share them.
  • Tracking — to measure advertising performance, as described in Section 3.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party SDKs. Each is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Adjust — attribution and analytics. Privacy policy: adjust.com/privacy-policy

6. Children

Margin Tarot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. International Users

By using Margin Tarot, you understand that any data processed by Adjust may be transferred to and processed in the United States, the European Union, or other jurisdictions where Adjust or its partners operate. Adjust states that it uses standard contractual clauses and other safeguards to protect such data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data. Because all personal data lives on your device, you can exercise these rights yourself through the app's Settings, or by contacting us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the change. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.
