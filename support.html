Getting Started

How do I start my first reading?

Open the app, tap the Reading tab on the bottom bar, choose a spread (Single Card, Three-Card, Five-Card Cross, Celtic Cross, or Love Spread), and write a question you'd like to sit with. Tap Begin and reveal the cards one by one — taking a moment with each before flipping the next.

How do I add my own physical deck?

Go to Library → Custom, tap the + button, and either take a photo with the camera or import one from your Photos library. Name the card, optionally add a note, and it will be stored in your personal collection — locally, on your device.

How does the daily card work?

Each day, the app draws one card for you automatically. You can find it on the Home tab. If you'd like a new card, open Settings → Daily Card → Draw a New Card for Today.

Data & Privacy

Where is my data stored?

All your journal entries, custom cards, and reading history are stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

How do I clear my data?

Open Settings → Data. You can remove custom cards, journal entries, or reset everything. Resetting removes all local content and draws a new daily card.

Does the app track me?

Margin Tarot uses the Adjust SDK to measure advertising performance. iOS will ask for your permission the first time the app launches, and you can change your choice anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Troubleshooting

The app crashes or freezes

First, make sure your iPhone is running the latest version of iOS supported by Margin Tarot and that the app itself is updated to the latest release. If the issue continues, please send an email to Mefepzavap@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and a description of what you were doing when the crash occurred.

I denied camera permission — can I still use the app?

Yes. Camera access is only needed when you choose to capture your own physical cards. Every other feature — library, daily card, readings, journal — works without it. You can grant permission later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → Margin Tarot.

I restored my iPhone from a backup and my data is gone

Margin Tarot stores data in iCloud Keychain–eligible local storage, so a same-device restore typically brings it back. A clean device setup, however, starts fresh — please journal or export anything important before resetting.
