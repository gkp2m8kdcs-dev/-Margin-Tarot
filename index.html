Complete 78-card library

Every card of the Major and Minor Arcana, each with hand-rendered design, upright and reversed meanings, keywords, astrological association, and a short affirmation.

◫

Five spread layouts

Single card, three-card line, five-card cross, ten-card Celtic Cross, and a focused love spread — each opens with your own question.

☀

Daily card practice

A single point of attention that rotates automatically each day. Sit with one card before the day decides itself.

◉

Custom card capture

Bring your own physical deck into the app: photograph a card or import an image, name it, add a note, and keep it in your private collection.

❖

Personal journal

Save each reading with a personal reflection for later. Your journal stays on your device and can be cleared at any time.

⌂

Local-only storage

No account, no sign-in, nothing leaves your device. Everything is stored locally and you stay in control of your data.
